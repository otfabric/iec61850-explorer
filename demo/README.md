# AA1 Northfield: the Explorer's demo substation

SCL files, a vendor namespace, packet captures and live IEDs of one small, made-up 110/20 kV substation, for demos, screenshots, screen recordings and tests of the IEC 61850 Explorer. Everything here is generated by the programs in this folder (`gen`, `ieds`) from one description of the substation (`internal/site`); names, addresses, APPIDs, VLANs and data sets agree across the SCD, the CIDs, the live IEDs and the captures. No file describes a real installation, vendor or product.

This folder is a Go module of its own (`github.com/otfabric/iec61850-explorer/demo`): it runs MMS servers and sends controls, which the Explorer's own module does not allow outside tests.

MMS domains are the IED name followed by the instance (`AA1D1Q02A1PROT`). Every IED's ConnectedAP gives OSI-PSEL 00000001, OSI-SSEL 0001, OSI-TSEL 0001, OSI-AP-Title 1,3,9999,33 and OSI-AE-Qualifier 33; the HMI of the captures connects with exactly these (go-iec61850 `ApplySCLAddress`).

**Feeders** (`AA1D1Q02A1`, `AA1D1Q03A1`), both the same function:

- `LD0`: LPHD1, GGIO1 (Ind1 SF6 pressure low, Ind2 spring charged, Ind3 trip circuit supervision).
- `PROT`: PTOC1 (I>, 51) and PTOC2 (I>>, 50) with StrVal and OpDlTmms in **two setting groups** (I> 600 A / 800 A, 300 ms / 500 ms; I>> 4000 A / 5000 A, 50 ms), PTRC1. `SettingControl numOfSGs="2" actSG="1"`. Data sets `dsTrip` (PTOC1/2.Str, PTOC1/2.Op, PTRC1.Tr, all FCD [ST]) and `dsGoTrip` (PTRC1.Tr.general, .q). Buffered RCB `brcbTrip` (rptID `<IED>PROT/LLN0.brcbTrip`, dchg qchg gi, seqNum dataSet reasonCode dataRef entryID configRef, bufTime 50). GoCB `gcbTrip`.
- `CTRL`: CSWI1 (breaker QA1) and CSWI2 (disconnector QB1), both **SBO with enhanced security** (sboTimeout 30 s); XCBR1, XSWI1 (SwTyp Disconnector); CILO1, CILO2 (interlocking). Data sets `dsPos` (CSWI1/2.Pos, XCBR1.Pos, XSWI1.Pos, XCBR1.OpCnt, CILO2.EnaOpn.stVal, CILO2.EnaCls.stVal) and `dsGoPos` (XCBR1.Pos.stVal, .q, .t). Buffered RCB `brcbPos`. LogControl `lcbEvents` on `dsPos` into the **journal** `EventLog`. GoCB `gcbPos`.
- `MEAS`: MMXU1 (every MMXU measurement: TotW, TotVAr, TotVA, TotPF, Hz, PPV, PNV, PhV, A, W, VAr, VA, PF, Z; deadbands), MSQI1 (SeqA), MMTR1 (TotWh, TotVArh, TotVAh, SupWh, SupVArh, DmdWh, DmdVArh). Data set `dsMeas`, unbuffered RCB `urcbMeas` (integrity 10 s, dchg qchg period gi).
- Every RCB has `RptEnabled max="2"` with `ClientLN` AA1KA1/LD0/IHMI1; `indexed="false"` (the IEDs serve one instance per RCB).

**Transformer** (`AA1E1Q01A1`): `PROT` PDIF1 (87T), PTOC1 (LV overcurrent, **subscribes** the feeders' `gcbTrip`: ExtRef BlkF1, BlkF2), PTRC1 (**subscribes** the busbar protection's `gcbBF`: BFTrip1, BFTrip2), `dsTrip`, `brcbTrip`. `CTRL` YPTR1, **YLTC1 with the vendor extension** (NamPlt.lnNs `CORVANE 7-4X:2026A`, data objects MotDrvTmp and MotDrvA with `dataNs`), ATCC1 (Auto: a direct control with normal security; BndCtr, BndWid), XCBR1 (HV), XCBR2 (LV incomer); `dsTap`, unbuffered `urcbTap`. `MEAS` MMXU1 (HV), MMXU2 (LV), MMXN1 (the tap changer's 230 V motor supply: Amp, Vol, Watt, VolAmpr, VolAmp, PwrFact, Hz), MHAI1 (harmonics: Hz), MMTR1 (LV energy), `dsMeas`, `urcbMeas`.

**Busbar protection** (`AA1D1W1A1`): `PROT` PDIF1 (87B, **subscribes** the merging unit's Sampled Values: ExtRef serviceType SMV to I TCTR1-3 AmpSv), RBRF1 and RBRF2 (breaker failure of feeder 1 and 2, FailTmms 150 ms, **subscribe** the feeders' `gcbTrip`), PTRC1; `dsGoBF` (RBRF1/2.OpEx.general, .q) for the GoCB `gcbBF`, `dsTrip` and `brcbTrip`.

**Merging unit** (`AA1D1Q02MU1`): LD `MU01` with LPHD1, ITCTR1-4 (AmpSv, ratings 600 A) and UTVTR1-4 (VolSv); data set `PhsMeas1` (the 9-2LE layout: 4 currents, 4 voltages, instMag.i and q each); SVCB `MSVCB01`; status GoCB `gcbStatus` on `dsStatus` (LPHD1.PhyHealth, LLN0.Beh).

### Search by quantity

The Explorer's search by quantity (frequency, voltage, current, active, reactive and apparent power, power factor, active, reactive and apparent energy, impedance) finds something for every one of them in feeders 1 and 2 and the transformer: every rule of go-iec61850-model's semantic mapping has a data object here. MMXU gives all three-phase quantities (voltages line to line `PPV`, line to neutral `PNV`, line to ground `PhV`; the impedance `Z`), MMXN the single-phase ones, MMTR the energies (total, supply and demand), and MHAI's `Hz` is found by the rule for `Hz` in any class. Only the values in `dsMeas` are reported; the others are read. Demo: open `scl/AA1_Northfield.scd`, choose the transformer, pick *Impedance* or *Active energy* in the search; or connect to a live IED and read what the search found.

## The day, and the captures

All captures are of 10 November 2026 (UTC), on the station bus mirror port. At **09:00:10.000** a two-phase fault (B-C) hits feeder 1: its I>> stage trips at 09:00:10.064 and publishes the trip by GOOSE, but its breaker QA1 does not open. The busbar protection's breaker failure function, started by that GOOSE, operates after 150 ms (09:00:10.216) and publishes breaker failure; the transformer protection, subscribed to it, trips the LV incomer, which opens at 09:00:10.268 and clears the fault. The 20 kV busbar is dead. At 09:20 the operator isolates feeder 1; at 09:30 the protection engineer looks at what happened. Feeder 1's file store holds the disturbance record of the fault (COMTRADE 1999, 1 kHz, the same waveform as the Sampled Values).

Counts below are go-mms-trace v0.3.0's (`internal/scenario/captures_test.go` checks them); "Explorer" lists what was checked by opening the files in the Explorer's engine (SCL sessions from the SCD, trace sessions linked to them).

### 01-hmi-browse-and-poll.pcapng (08:55)

The HMI associates with feeder 1, the transformer and the busbar protection in turn: Identify, GetNameList (domains, then the variables of every logical device), GetNamedVariableListAttributes of every data set, GetVariableAccessAttributes of what it displays, a first read, then three poll cycles 2 s apart (Read of several variables at once), and closes all three (Conclude, then release).
251 frames; 3 associations (associate/release); 69 transactions, all ok: GetNameList 29, GetNamedVariableListAttributes 10, GetVariableAccessAttributes 15, Identify 3, Read 12.

*Demo:* open the SCD, choose AA1D1Q02A1 (an SCL session); open the capture; link it to that session. Associations and the timeline show the discovery; the objects of feeder 1 resolve to IEC 61850 references, the 20 objects of the transformer and the busbar protection are `not-in-model` warnings (a trace links to one IED's model: link it to AA1E1Q01A1 to see the others resolve).

### 02-reports.pcapng (08:57)

Feeder 1: the HMI reads `urcbMeas`, reserves it (Resv), sets IntgPd 2000 ms, enables it, asks for a GI; a load change follows (TotW, TotVAr, Hz, A, SeqA: one data-change report each; the scenario changes them one at a time and waits for each report), then an integrity report 2 s after enabling. Then `brcbPos`: read, PurgeBuf, enable, GI; the disconnector's position supervision goes invalid|inconsistent and back (two quality-change reports). Both RCBs are disabled and the URCB released.
69 frames; 1 association; 15 transactions ok (Write 10, Read 2, GetNamedVariableListAttributes 2, Identify 1); 10 reports.

*Demo:* link to AA1D1Q02A1 from the SCD (or its CID): all 10 reports are decoded against `dsMeas` and `dsPos`, with data references and reasons (GI, data-change, quality-change, integrity).

### 03-control-sbo.pcapng (09:20)

Open QB1 (CSWI2): SBOw accepted, Oper refused: **LastApplError** (AddCause blocked-by-interlocking) before the negative Write response; the HMI cancels. 2. Open QA1 (CSWI1): SBOw, Oper, the position reports (OpCnt, XCBR1.Pos, CSWI1.Pos, CILO2 EnaOpn and EnaCls) and the **CommandTermination**. 3. ## Live IEDs

```bash
make demo-ieds                # from the repository root, or: cd demo && go run ./ieds
```

Serves feeder 1, feeder 2, the transformer, the busbar protection and the merging unit on `127.0.0.1:10201` to `10205` (`-base-port`, `-host`), from their CIDs on go-iec61850's server. Ctrl-C stops them. With the daemon: `bin/iec61850-explorerd --allow-target 127.0.0.1`.

- **Measurements** change every 2 s (load and frequency drift); the transformer's voltage controller moves the tap changer every 44 s while `ATCC1.Auto` is on. Enable `urcbMeas` or `urcbTap` in Commission mode to watch reports arrive.
- **Controls** (Commission mode, capability `controls`): feeder QA1 (`CTRL/CSWI1.Pos`) and QB1 (`CTRL/CSWI2.Pos`) are SBO with enhanced security; the breaker reports position, operation counter and interlocking changes, and the CommandTermination follows. **QB1 is interlocked**: it refuses (blocked-by-interlocking) while QA1 is closed; open QA1 first. Closing QA1 with QB1 open is refused too. `AA1E1Q01A1CTRL/ATCC1.Auto` is a direct control with normal security.
- **Setting groups**: feeder PROT has two; selecting the active group (capability `setting-groups`) switches the overcurrent settings.
- **Files**: each IED serves its CID; feeder 1 also the COMTRADE record of the 09:00:10 fault.
- **Journal**: feeders' `CTRL/EventLog`, with last night's switching and every operation since the start.
- **Compare (SCL against IED)**: feeder 1 deliberately differs from its CID: firmware `LD0/LPHD1.PhyNam.swRev` 2.4.1 (the CID says 2.4.0, someone updated the relay) and `PROT/PTOC1.StrVal.setMag.f` 650 A in setting group 1 (the CID says 600 A, someone changed it on the front panel). Open `scl/AA1D1Q02A1.cid`, connect to 127.0.0.1:10201 and compare with values: exactly these two differences. `-exact` serves it as the CID says.
- **Edition 1 and namespaces**: open `scl/AA1D1Q03A1.cid`: its logical devices declare no namespace and the file is schema 1.7, so they are checked as Edition 1 (live, on 10202, nothing says Edition 1: the check uses the default profile). In the SCD, the same IED is an Edition 2 file's IED without declarations. Open the transformer: YLTC1 declares `CORVANE 7-4X:2026A`, a warning "neither embedded nor loaded"; load `nsd/corvane-7-4x-2026a.nsd` as a namespace package and check again: no finding, MotDrvTmp and MotDrvA are defined there.

GOOSE and Sampled Values are not published live (that needs raw Ethernet and privileges); they are in the captures.

The output is the same on every run: fixed times (the scenario clocks start at 08:55, 08:57, 09:20, 09:30, 09:00:05, 09:00:09.9, 09:00:04 and 10:00), fixed seeds for latencies, TCP sequence numbers and IP IDs, and no value from the host's clock in any file.

The tests (`go test ./...` here) parse and validate the SCD and every CID with go-iec61850 `scl`, build a server model of every IED from both (no warnings), bind every GoCB and the SVCB from the SCD, serve and discover every IED, run the controls, the interlock, the setting groups, the file store and the journal against the live runtime, and analyse every capture with go-mms-trace against the counts above.

## How the captures are made

- **MMS** is real traffic: the IEDs are go-iec61850 v1.19.0 servers built from the CIDs (`NewServerModelFromSCL`), the HMI is a go-iec61850 client (go-mms v1.9.0), on TCP over loopback through a relay that records each TPKT in both directions before it forwards it. The recorded units are framed again as Ethernet/IPv4/TCP between the SCD's addresses (port 102, the HMI on ephemeral ports from 49732), with a handshake, consistent sequence and acknowledgement numbers, delayed ACKs, checksums and a FIN close. Times come from the scenario's clock: a response 1.2 to 4.8 ms after its request, the next request 0.3 to 1.1 ms after the response, and the scenario's own pauses.
- **GOOSE and SV** frames are encoded by go-iec61850's `goose` and `sv` packages from the control blocks `goose.ControlBlocksFromSCL` and `sv.ControlBlocksFromSCL` read from the SCD (destination, APPID, VLAN, gocbRef, datSet, goID, confRev, MinTime, MaxTime, svID, smpRate, the 9-2LE layout), with the source MACs above, on the scenario's clock: the GOOSE timing is go-iec61850's publisher's (first retransmission after MinTime, doubling to MaxTime), SV every 250 us.
- The **problems** of capture 08 are made by the capture point and the relay (left-out frames, a muted start, a relay that stops forwarding, one corrupted request), never by editing captured bytes otherwise.
- Workarounds, each because of a gap below: the HMI builds the `Oper`/`SBOw`/`Cancel` structures itself (IEC 61850-8-1 layout, T from the scenario clock) and writes them with go-iec61850's `Client.Write`; the IEDs set the parts of a data object together and notify once; changes that each cause a report are made one at a time (paced by 30 ms in the IED when they come from a control); the SGCB's LActTm is stamped again with the IED's clock after a change; the order of the two TCP closes is fixed (client first). go-iec61850 v1.18 closed gaps 1 and 2 of the first version of this list (BufTm, an injectable clock); the demo keeps these workarounds, and its captures are byte-identical with go-iec61850 v1.19.0. The live IEDs use go-iec61850's `OperateWait` path through the Explorer and need none of this.

## Gaps found (reported, not patched)

1. **go-iec61850 v1.16.1 server: FCD change test.** For an FCD member the change test compares the whole structure: a time stamp written alone already triggers a data-change report (not re-checked since v1.16.1).
2. **Explorer (this repository): no SGCB from SCL.** A trace linked to an SCL session reports the SGCB (`LLN0$SP$SGCB`) as `not-in-model` although the SCL's LLN0 has a SettingControl (the engine builds no setting group control block from SCL). Also, a trace links to one IED's model: captures with several IEDs show the others' objects as `not-in-model`.

Fixed since the demo was made: go-iec61850 v1.18.0 applies BufTm (IEC 61850-7-2 §17.2.2.9; v1.16.1 reported every change after a fixed 100 ms, one report per change) and stamps Oper.T and Cancel's T, the SGCB's LActTm, a BRCB's TimeOfEntry, the report's timeStamp and journal entries from an injectable `Clock` (`ServerOptions.Clock`, `ClientOptions.Clock`; v1.16.1 used `time.Now` with no option). go-iec61850 v1.17 sends the reports of an association in order, through one queue per association (they left from a goroutine each, in any order); go-mms v1.8.0 rejects a request it cannot decode with its invoke ID when that can be read and without one otherwise, its client hands a Reject without an invoke ID to the only outstanding request instead of leaving it to time out, and an unsupported service gets a Reject (with v1.7.1 the Reject carried invoke ID 0 and capture 08 had an `orphan`). Also: go-iec61850 v1.16.1 reads `type` before `xsi:type` (it took the address type from `xsi:type`, losing IP and MAC addresses) and applies the schema default of optional booleans such as TrgOps `gi` and OptFields `bufOvfl` (an absent `gi` read false, and an Edition 1 file never had GI).
